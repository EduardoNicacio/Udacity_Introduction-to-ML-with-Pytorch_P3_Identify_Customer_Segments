# Reviewer Notes

## Congratulation

The revision resolves the preprocessing-consistency issue identified in the previous review. The customer and population datasets now share the same definition of row quality, the quality decision is carried safely into the engineered customer feature space, and the transformations learned from the general population are reused consistently for the customer data.

Considering the **Identify Customer Segments project as a whole**, this type of workflow maps closely to practical customer segmentation and marketing analytics. A company can learn demographic segments from a broad reference population, locate its existing customers within those segments, identify groups that are disproportionately represented among customers, and use those findings to form targeting hypotheses for future campaigns. Keeping preprocessing identical between the reference population and customer data is particularly important in production because otherwise apparent differences between segments can be caused by the data pipeline rather than genuine demographic differences.
